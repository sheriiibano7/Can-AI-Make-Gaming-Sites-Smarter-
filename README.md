<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">For gaming sites, that can mean a smarter operation from top to bottom. It&apos;s not necessarily about sticking a chatbot on the homepage and calling it AI. The more interesting stuff is happening underneath.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Getting Personal</span></strong></p>

<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">One obvious use is personalisation.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Instead of showing everyone exactly the same games and offers, AI can look at how different customers use a site and help segment them into different groups. That can then feed into game recommendations, targeted offers and CRM campaigns.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">The same idea can be used for bonuses and promotional activity, with systems identifying different customer segments and deciding which offers are most appropriate for each one.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Keeping an Eye Out for Fraud</span></strong></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">This is probably one of the less glamorous uses of AI, but potentially one of the most useful.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Gaming sites deal with plenty of transactions and a lot of customer activity, so spotting unusual behaviour matters. AI systems can look for things such as multiple accounts, strange payment activity and patterns that don&apos;t look quite right.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Rather than relying entirely on someone noticing something odd, automated systems can flag suspicious activity much faster.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">That doesn&apos;t mean AI gets the final say on everything. It can simply give operators another set of eyes.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Sportsbooks Have Plenty of Numbers</span></strong></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Sports betting is another obvious area for AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Odds are constantly moving, particularly during live events, and operators need to keep track of what is happening across thousands of markets. AI can analyse incoming information and help with live odds adjustments and risk management.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">That&apos;s a lot of data for humans to process manually.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">AI can get through it considerably quicker, helping sportsbooks manage their exposure while events are actually taking place.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Less Clicking, More Automation</span></strong></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Then there is CRM.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Emails, push notifications and other customer communications can all be triggered automatically according to what is happening on an account. Someone who hasn&apos;t visited for a while might enter one type of campaign, while another customer could receive different messaging based on their activity.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Again, it&apos;s about taking a mountain of repetitive work and letting software handle it.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Customer support is another obvious candidate.&nbsp;</span><a href="https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/generative-ai/how-ai-code-generation-works/"><u><span style="color:#1155cc;font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">AI chatbots</span></u></a><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">&nbsp;can deal with straightforward questions, while automated systems can sort and handle basic support tickets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">That doesn&apos;t mean every customer suddenly wants to talk to a robot. But for simple queries, it can save everyone a bit of time.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">AI and Responsible Gambling</span></strong></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Perhaps the most important use is&nbsp;</span><a href="https://esgigaming.com/blog/ai-in-responsible-gambling/"><u><span style="color:#1155cc;font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">responsible gambling</span></u></a><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">AI can analyse behaviour and identify patterns that could indicate risky gambling activity. That can help operators spot potential concerns earlier and support their responsible gambling and compliance processes.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">This is an area where getting the balance right is particularly important. AI should be another tool for identifying potentially problematic behaviour, rather than some magical system that understands exactly what a person is thinking.</span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Smarter Marketing Too</span></strong></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Finally, there is marketing.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">AI can analyse traffic quality, customer segments and campaign performance, helping operators work out where their marketing is actually doing its job.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">So, can AI make gaming sites smarter?</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Quite possibly. From fraud detection and sportsbook analytics to customer support, marketing and responsible gambling, there are plenty of areas where it can take a huge amount of data and turn it into something more useful.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">The clever bit isn&apos;t necessarily what customers can see.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">It&apos;s everything happening in the background while they&apos;re using the site.</span></p>
